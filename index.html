Genetics Guess Who

How to Play

One hidden child has been selected by the computer.

Your goal is to identify the correct child by asking yes/no questions about the child's parents.

Use the question controls on the right side of the screen. After receiving answers, eliminate children that can no longer be the correct choice.

When you believe you know the answer, select a child and click "Guess Selected Child".

Educational Disclaimer

This game uses simplified inheritance models for educational purposes only.

Real human traits such as hair colour, eye colour, face shape, freckles, glasses use, and nose shape do not inherit in the simple ways shown here.

Most real traits are influenced by many genes and environmental factors. The inheritance patterns shown in this game are intended only to demonstrate genetics concepts.
