Genetics Guess Who

How to Play / Játékszabályok

In this game, one child is secretly selected from a group of 20. Your goal is to identify that child using logic and genetics.

You do NOT guess randomly. Instead, you ask yes/no questions about the child's parents to deduce which child must be correct.

Example questions: "Does the father have blue eyes?" "Does the mother have freckles?"

Each child has traits inherited from their parents using simplified genetic rules.

Genetics Explanation / Genetikai alapok

Dominant trait (domináns tulajdonság): If present, it appears in the phenotype even if only one allele is inherited.

Recessive trait (recesszív tulajdonság): Only appears if both inherited alleles are recessive.

Incomplete dominance (részleges dominancia): Both alleles influence the trait, producing a blended result.

Codominance (kodominancia): Both alleles are expressed simultaneously.

Multiple alleles (többszörös allélok): More than two allele variants exist for a trait.

Important / Fontos

This model is simplified for educational use. Real human genetics is far more complex and influenced by many genes and environmental factors.
